I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics, Econometrics and Finance at the University of Groningen under the supervision of Dr. Pim Heijnen and Dr. Zsombor Méder after September 2025. I have a Master’s degree in Applied Cognitive Psychology from the University of Utrecht.  Leveraging my experience in methodology development in applied psychology, I approach economic problems through the lens of a behavioral scientist.

My doctoral research is centered on the project: Decision Making in Generative AI: Rationality, Bias & Benchmark. Through this appointment, I aim to make meaningful contributions to the evolving methodology of machine behavior (Rahwan et al., 2019).

### Research Interests

*   **Delegating to LLMs**
    > The increasing usage on LLMs for consultation and task delegation introduces a dynamic new class of principal-agent problems. Human principals cannot *ex ante* specify preferences for every scenario an LLM agent might encounter. Besides, how a bystander view this task delegation? This is to explore not only the micro-level human-LLM interactions but also their broader societal and market-level implications.

*   **Emergent rationality of LLMs**
    > As we increasingly utilize LLMs in decision-making experiments, benchmarking their economic rationality is essential. A key focus is exploring robust methodologies for deploying LLMs as reliable subjects or agents in laboratory environments.

*   **Information filtering using LLMs**
    > Beyond simple task delegation, humans can leverage LLMs for their decision making by exploiting the models' superior capacity for memory storage, rapid search, and information categorization. I am interested on how individuals can utilize these tools to process complex information and improve their strategic decision-making.

*   **Behavioral economics and Choice theory**
    > I am broadly interested in foundational topics within behavioral economics, including binary choice theory, social preferences, decision-making under risk, and intertemporal choice. I aim to bridge these classical frameworks with the evolving landscape of AI agents.
